# Contributing

Thanks for stopping by.

This repo is a personal scratch pad, a living collection of patterns, guidelines, and hard-won lessons accumulated over time. It draws on the author's own experience and the published wisdom of others. It is not comprehensive, not authoritative, and unfinished (by design).

**Contributions aren't open here.** Please do not create issues or submit pull requests.

Not because your ideas aren't good; they probably are. This is a space to pressure-test personal hypotheses, validate _(or invalidate)_ half-formed opinions, and evolve thinking without the overhead of consensus.

If something here sparks an idea, run with it in your own repo. That's the right venue for it.

Appreciate you reading this far.
